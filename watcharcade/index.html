In English

WatchArcade turns your Apple Watch into a motion controller for five quick mini-games on iPhone: slice ingredients in Chop Chop Chef, fling shuriken in Shuriken Rush, lean through curves in Rail Rider, reel in the big one in Fishing King and blast through space in Rocket Rumble. Pick a nickname and climb weekly and all-time leaderboards worldwide, in your country or near you. No watch? Every game also works with touch.
